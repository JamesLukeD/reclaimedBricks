    The differences are: Wirecut Bricks

  • Made by cutting clay with wires after it’s pushed through a mould by hand.
  • Smooth, straight and uniform finish.
  • Versatile have a lot of character, but their uniformity makes them easy to work with.
    • Handmade Bricks
  • Oldest form of brick making.
  • Made using clay which is shaped by hand in moulds.
  • Rustic, uneven finish with lots of character – each brick is unique.
  • Perfect for period homes, fireplaces, or anywhere you would like a traditional, aged look.
    • Pressed Bricks
  • Made using clay which is pressed into moulds and compressed down by a machine.
  • Neater look and finish than a handmade brick.
  • Normally have a frog mark bearing the maker’s name.
  • Ideal for Victorian homes, outdoor walls and general building use as pressed bricks are a lot harder than handmade bricks.
  • Normally used for engineering purposes such as foundations and supporting walls.
